# SD Preparation

Power off your 3DS and remove the SD Card. Insert it into your computer, phone or other device that can read SD Cards.

## Conflicting files

::: warning

While there are safety measures in place to prevent any conflicts, we can never 100% guarantee that other services will prevent them. We recommend only having Cirrus (Samtendo Network's switcher) installed at the same time. You are responsible for any conflicts.

:::

## Installation

### Using Universal-Updater

Open Universal-Updater, open the search menu, search for Cirrus and choose CIA or 3DSX. You do not need to do anything else, as Universal-Updater does it for you.

### Manually

Download **one** of the ZIPs [from here](https://github.com/SamtendoNetwork/cirrus/releases/latest), then follow the respective instructions.

- 3dsx.zip
- cia.zip
- combined.zip

#### CIA

Extract the ZIP. Place the `cirrus.cia` file in `SD:/cias/` (if that folder doesn't exist, create it). Open the `3ds` folder. Place the `cirrus` folder in `SD:/3ds/` too.

Put your SD card back into your console, turn it on and open FBI. Choose `SD`, then `cias`, `cirrus.cia` and finally `Install and delete CIA`.

#### 3DSX

Extract the ZIP. Place the `cirrus.3dsx` file in `SD:/3ds/`. Open the `3ds` folder. Place the `cirrus` folder in `SD:/3ds/` too.

#### Combined

Combine the instructions for CIA and 3DSX.

Once you're done, move on to **Finalising Setup**.
